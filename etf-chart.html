ETF PERFORMANCE

How Have Some ETFs Performed?

This chart shows the yearly return of some popular ETFs. A positive number means the ETF went up that year, while a negative number means it went down.

2025 vs 2022

2025 was a positive year for these ETFs, while 2022 was a difficult year for the stock market.




VOO

VOO follows the S&P 500, which includes many large U.S. companies.

2025: +17.82%

2022: -18.17%

VTI

VTI covers a large part of the U.S. stock market, including large, medium and smaller companies.

2025: +17.10%

2022: -19.52%

QQQ

QQQ follows the Nasdaq-100 and has many technology and other large companies.

2025: +20.77%

2022: -32.58%

SPY

SPY also follows the S&P 500 and is one of the most well-known ETFs.

2025: +17.72%

2022: -18.18%

VT

VT contains companies from around the world instead of only focusing on the United States.

2025: +22.44%

2022: -18.01%

WHAT THE CHART SHOWS

ETFs can go up and down.

Looking at the chart, you can see that even popular ETFs can lose money.

For example, QQQ lost about 32.6% in 2022. This shows that ETFs still have risk.

Just because an ETF contains lots of companies does not mean that it will always make money.
