SPREAD YOUR MONEY

Don't put all your money in one place.

One of the reasons people like ETFs is because they can spread their money across many different companies and investments.

Why does spreading your money matter?

Imagine you own stock in only one company. If something goes wrong with that company, your entire investment could be affected.

1

One company

If all your money is in one company, that company has a very big effect on your investment.

2

Many companies

An ETF can contain many companies. This means your money is spread around instead of depending on only one company.

3

Still has risk

Spreading your money does not mean you can't lose money. ETFs can still go down.

SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Think about your eggs.

Imagine you have 10 eggs and put all of them in one basket.

If you drop the basket, all 10 eggs could break.

If you put the eggs into several baskets, dropping one basket wouldn't necessarily break all of them.

This is basically the idea behind spreading your money.

See how ETFs have actually performed

Remember that spreading your money can reduce some risks, but it doesn't remove risk completely.
