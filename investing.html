HOW INVESTING WORKS

What actually happens when you invest?

Investing means putting money into something that you hope will become worth more in the future. However, there is always a chance that it could lose value instead.

Three things to understand

1

Prices change

Stock and ETF prices can change every day. They can go up when people want to buy more and go down when people want to sell.

2

You can make money

If an investment becomes more valuable and you sell it for more than you paid, you can make a profit.

3

You can lose money

If an investment becomes less valuable, you could lose money if you sell it for less than you paid.

LONG TERM

Investing isn't supposed to be a quick game.

Many investors hold their investments for years instead of trying to make money every single day.

The stock market can have good years and bad years. This is one reason why understanding what you are investing in is important.

ETFs are one option people use when they want to invest in many companies at once.
