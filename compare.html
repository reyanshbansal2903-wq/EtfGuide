ETF VS STOCKS

Should you buy one stock or an ETF?

Both choices have advantages and disadvantages. The biggest difference is how many companies your money is invested in.

One Company's Stock

  • Your money depends heavily on one company.
  • If the company does really well, your investment could grow a lot.
  • If the company performs badly, you could lose a lot of money.
  • You have to research that company yourself.

POPULAR CHOICE

ETF

  • One ETF can contain many companies.
  • Your money is spread between different investments.
  • One company's bad performance may have less of an effect on the entire ETF.
  • You can invest in a large group of companies with one purchase.

MY EXAMPLE

What if one company has a bad year?

Imagine you put $1,000 into one company. Then the company has a terrible year and its stock drops 30%.

Your $1,000 would become about $700.

If you had your money spread across many companies, one company having a bad year may not affect all of your money as much.
