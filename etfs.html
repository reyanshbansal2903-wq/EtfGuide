WHAT ARE ETFS?

ETFs are like a basket of investments.

Think of an ETF like a basket. Instead of buying one company's stock, the basket can contain pieces of many different companies.

How does an ETF work?

When you buy one share of an ETF, your money is being used to own a small part of all the investments inside that ETF.

1

You buy the ETF

You can buy an ETF through a brokerage account, just like you can buy a normal stock.

2

The ETF owns investments

The ETF holds many different investments inside it depending on what the ETF is designed to follow.

3

You own a small part

Your ETF share represents a small piece of all those investments.

SIMPLE EXAMPLE

Think about pizza.

Imagine you want to try different kinds of pizza. You could buy one whole pizza that only has cheese.

Or you could buy a pizza with several different toppings. You get a little bit of everything.

ETFs work in a similar way. One ETF can give you pieces of many different companies.

But is an ETF better than one stock?

It depends on your goals and how much risk you are comfortable with.
