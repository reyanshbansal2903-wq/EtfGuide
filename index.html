THE BASICS

So, what exactly is an ETF?

An ETF is a group of investments that you can buy all at once. Instead of buying only one company, an ETF can let you own small parts of many companies.

1

Many companies

One ETF can contain lots of different companies. This means your money isn't all depending on one company.

2

Easy to buy

ETFs trade on the stock market just like regular stocks, so they can be bought and sold through many investing apps.

3

Spread your money

Instead of putting everything into one company, you can spread your money across many different companies.

WHY IT MATTERS

Don't put everything in one place.

Imagine you put all of your money into one company. If that company has a really bad year, your money could lose a lot of value.

An ETF can help you spread your money between many companies instead.
